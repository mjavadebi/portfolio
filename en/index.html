01 / PLATFORM & GROWTH

Modiran Click

↗

From a service portal to a platform for customers and business partners.

When I took it on, Modiran Click was mainly an after-sales portal. With the team, I helped rebrand and expand it into a platform with room for sales, insurance and value for Modiran Khodro’s business partners.

Through UX research and simpler sign-up and identity verification, we helped newly registered users complete their requests more successfully: the rate rose from 65% to 92%.

For initial payments after the sales draw, we introduced a way to split a large payment order into smaller amounts so customers could pay from multiple accounts. Payment success rose from 70% to 86%.

02 / INSURANCE ECOSYSTEM

Insurance, from back office to storefront

↗

To improve a process, first you have to see how the work really happens.

Before building the back office, I went into the organisation and spoke with managers and specialists to understand how their work actually flowed. Together, we designed and implemented the process from registration to issuance.

Internet disruptions and the 12-day war also made continuity part of the problem. The redesigned process reduced registration-to-issuance time from a previous average of one hour to 10 minutes.

After the back office came Mybimco for capturing leads, followed by direct insurance sales through Modiran Click: three parts of one journey, each solving a different problem.
